01 / CIRCUITS

SIGNALS, SYSTEMS & SMALL WONDERS

Electronics

I love how a few components and a curious question can become something useful, surprising, or both.

From reading a schematic to tracing a stubborn signal, every board has a story hidden in its paths.

02 / TRANSFORMATIONS

MATTER IN MOTION

Chemistry

I’m drawn to the invisible changes happening all around us—and the experiments that let us see them unfold.

Color shifts, crystals, reactions: chemistry makes the ordinary feel like a very good magic trick.

03 / EXPRESSION
MAKE
SPACE

A DIFFERENT WAY OF SEEING

Art

A place to play, notice, and make a mark. Art keeps my technical side company without asking it to explain everything.

Sometimes the point is the process: trying a color, following a line, and seeing what happens next.

04 / PATTERNS
LEARN → ADAPT

LEARNING FROM EXAMPLES

AI & machine learning

I’m curious about how machines find patterns, learn from data, and help people solve problems in new ways.

From a model’s inner workings to the choices that shape its results, there’s always another layer to explore.

05 / THE WEB

FROM IDEA TO INTERFACE

Web development

I enjoy turning ideas into experiences people can use, one thoughtful detail and working interaction at a time.

A satisfying mix of logic and creativity can make a page feel clear, useful, and alive.

06 / CONNECTIONS

Networking

I like learning how devices communicate, how information travels, and what keeps a connection reliable.

There’s an invisible architecture behind the simple feeling of being connected.

07 / EVIDENCE

Data science

Good questions get even more interesting when you can explore the evidence and uncover a useful pattern.

I enjoy the path from messy information to a clearer picture of what might be going on.
